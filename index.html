Wait... what actually is AI?

Artificial intelligence is technology that finds patterns in information and uses them to make predictions or create answers. It can recognize speech and suggest videos and write text.

🧩

Think of AI as a pattern finder

Imagine showing someone thousands of pictures of cats and dogs. Over time they learn what makes each animal different. AI can learn patterns from examples too. But it can still make mistakes.

03 / THE BIG PICTURE

Super helpful. Not always right.

AI has real benefits but using it responsibly matters.

✓

Why people love it

  • Learning: It can explain difficult ideas in simple words
  • Accessibility: It can turn speech into text
  • Healthcare: It can help doctors examine medical images
  • Creativity: It can help brainstorm and draft ideas
!

What can go wrong

  • Wrong answers: It can sound confident and still be incorrect
  • Bias: Unfair training data can lead to unfair results
  • Privacy: Personal information needs protection
  • Deepfakes: Fake voices and videos can trick people
REAL WORLD EXAMPLE

What if everyone in a video call looks real?

In Hong Kong scammers used fake video meeting participants to convince an employee to transfer money. The lesson is simple: a realistic video is not proof that someone is who they say they are.

04 / WHAT'S NEXT?

The future is exciting... and complicated

These are possibilities rather than promises.

🚀

Smarter helpers

AI could make studying and work easier with more personalized tools.

⚡

More electricity

AI data centers need power. Rapid growth could strain local grids if planning does not keep up. Blackouts are not guaranteed.

🛡️

More safety checks

AI systems can be attacked and online services can go offline. Human review and backup plans will remain important.

06 / BEHIND THE SCENES

How this website was made

I wanted to make a website that feels friendly for someone who has never learned about AI before. I organized it like a short journey starting with the basics and moving into everyday examples and bigger questions about safety and the future. I chose dark forest green with a cream background because I wanted the site to feel calm and easy to read. I kept the layout simple and used cards to separate the information.

I used HTML to organize the sections and CSS to style the page and make it fit smaller screens. JavaScript makes the cards open and powers the short quiz. The page uses text and CSS shapes instead of downloaded images so it loads quickly.

I used ChatGPT to help with the first draft of the writing and code. I reviewed the text and added links to sources that readers can check. I also need to test the links and quiz on my phone and computer before I submit the project.
