01 / THE BASICS

What actually is AI?

Artificial intelligence is technology that can recognize patterns, make predictions and create things like text or images.

AI learns from information and examples. It does not think exactly like a person and it can still make mistakes.

Think of it like this 🧩

Imagine showing someone thousands of pictures of cats and dogs. Eventually they learn how to tell them apart. AI can learn patterns from examples in a similar way.

1. Data

AI uses information and examples.

2. Patterns

It finds connections in that information.

3. Output

It makes a prediction or creates an answer.

4. Check

People should check important results.

03 / GOOD AND BAD

AI is helpful but not perfect

✓ Benefits

Education: Makes learning easier.

Healthcare: Helps doctors analyze information.

Accessibility: Helps people communicate.

Creativity: Helps generate new ideas.

! Risks

Mistakes: AI can give wrong answers.

Bias: Some results can be unfair.

Privacy: Personal data may be exposed.

Deepfakes: Fake videos can fool people.

Fraud: Scammers can impersonate others.

Dependence: People may stop checking things themselves.

Real example: Deepfake scams

Scammers have used AI-generated video calls to pretend to be real coworkers. This shows why we should verify unusual requests before sending money or private information.

04 / THE FUTURE

What could happen next?

🚀 Smarter tools

AI could help people study, work and communicate more easily.

⚡ Electricity demand

AI data centers use electricity. Rapid growth could put pressure on power grids and increase the risk of outages in some places if infrastructure does not keep up.

🔒 Security

AI can help detect threats but can also be misused by hackers and scammers.

👥 Human supervision

AI services can have outages or fail. People still need to make important decisions and have backup plans.

My opinion 💭

I think AI is useful when it helps us rather than replaces our thinking. We should enjoy its benefits while understanding its limits.

06 / BEHIND THE WEBSITE

How this website was made

I wanted to make a website about artificial intelligence that anyone could understand. My main idea was to explain AI without using too many complicated words. I started with a simple definition and then added examples from everyday life so people could relate to the topic.

I chose dark forest green and cream because I wanted the website to look simple and calm. I organized the information into short sections so visitors would not feel overwhelmed. I also included clickable cards and a short quiz to make learning more interesting.

HTML was used for the content and structure. CSS was used for the colors, spacing and mobile layout. JavaScript was used to make the cards and quiz interactive. I used ChatGPT to help draft explanations and create code. I included sources so visitors could learn more and check the information.
